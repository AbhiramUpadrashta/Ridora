Three ways to share a journey

🚗🏍️

Offer a ride

Driving to Vijayawada on Saturday? Riding to office every morning? Publish your car or bike trip, set a fair price per seat and fill your empty seats. Add stopovers, repeat it for your daily commute, and choose instant booking or approve each request.

🚕🛺

Share a cab or auto

Booking a cab to the airport or an auto to the station? Post it and let people heading the same way join and split the fare. Ridora suggests a fair share for everyone.

🙋

Request a ride

Can’t find a ride on your route? Post a request with your date and budget. Drivers searching that route see you and can offer you a seat in one tap.

How it works

For passengers

  1. SearchType where you’re leaving from and going to — any city, area, landmark or exact address — then pick a day and the number of seats.
  2. Pick your rideSee times, price, the route on a map, the driver’s rating, vehicle and preferences. Rides passing near you show how far the pickup is.
  3. BookInstant-booking rides confirm right away; others send a request. You get the driver’s number once confirmed.
  4. Travel & rateChat inside Ridora, share your live location with family, pay by UPI or cash, then rate your driver.

For drivers & bikers

  1. PublishChoose car or bike, set start, destination and stopovers. Ridora draws the route and estimates distance and time.
  2. Set your priceGet a fair-price suggestion per seat based on distance. Share costs, not profits.
  3. Accept passengersSee each passenger’s profile and rating. Accept, decline, call or message — all from your ride page.
  4. Get paid directlyPassengers pay you by UPI or cash. Ridora takes no commission.

Drivers and passengers rate each other after every trip. Read reviews before you book.

Questions

Is Ridora free?

Yes. No booking fees, no commission and no ads. Passengers pay drivers directly by UPI or cash. Prices are meant to share fuel and toll costs, not to make a profit.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Ridora runs in your phone’s browser. Add it to your home screen (Chrome: “Install app”, Safari: Share → “Add to Home Screen”) to open it like an app.

Why only a mobile number — is it verified?

You sign up with your mobile number, a username and a password — no email. Ridora doesn’t send SMS codes, so numbers aren’t SMS-verified. Use ratings, reviews and a quick call before your trip to build trust.

How does Ridora work without a company server?

Rides, bookings and ratings are signed records stored on public Nostr relays, so there’s no central Ridora database. Your account key is created from your number and password, which is why you can sign in on any device — and why you should keep your recovery code safe.

I forgot my password.

Use the recovery code you saved when you signed up. Without it, nobody can reset the password.

Where do addresses and maps come from?

Address search uses OpenStreetMap data via Photon, routes come from OSRM, and maps from OpenStreetMap and CARTO. You can also drop a pin on the map for an exact pickup point.

Do I get notified about bookings?

Yes, while Ridora is open: you see a pop-up and, if you allow it, a browser notification. Keep Ridora open (or installed on your home screen) around your trip time.
