# Ridora — share the ride

Free carpooling and ride sharing in your browser: offer seats in your car or on your bike, share a cab or auto, or request a ride. Sign up with just your mobile number — no email, no fees.

- **Open Ridora:** https://abhiramupadrashta.github.io/Ridora/app/
- **Website:** https://abhiramupadrashta.github.io/Ridora/

Rides, bookings, ratings and encrypted messages are stored on public Nostr relays — there is no Ridora server. Maps © OpenStreetMap contributors & CARTO, address search by Photon, routes by OSRM.
